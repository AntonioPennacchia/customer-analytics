# Dataset

## Overview

This folder contains the synthetic datasets used for Wossist customer analytics projects.

The datasets simulate a realistic insurance customer environment and represent the main business processes described in the company overview.

---

## Available Tables

### Customers

Customer master data.

Represents the customer base of Wossist.

---

### Products

Insurance product catalog.

Includes the products offered by Wossist across different business lines.

---

### Campaigns

Marketing campaign information.

Contains campaign objectives, products involved and campaign details.

---

### Customer Interactions

Customer engagement events generated through marketing and communication activities.

Examples:

- Email
- Phone
- SMS
- Digital interactions

---

### Quotes

Customer requests for insurance quotes.

Used to analyze customer interest and conversion funnel.

---

### Policies

Purchased insurance policies.

Represents successful customer conversions.

---

### Renewals

Renewal events for recurring insurance products.

Used for retention and churn analysis.
