# Insurance Customer Analytics

## Overview

This case study simulates a customer analytics environment within an insurance company.

The project is based on **Wossist**, a fictional insurance and assistance company created to reproduce a realistic business scenario and demonstrate how customer data can support business decisions.

The objective is to build a complete customer analytics ecosystem, from business understanding and data modeling to customer insights and reporting.

---

## Company Overview

**Wossist** is a fictional Italian insurance and assistance company operating in the protection and insurance market.

The company provides insurance solutions across three main business areas:

- Travel Insurance
- Pet Insurance
- Home Insurance

Wossist serves retail customers through multiple sales channels, combining digital distribution, agencies, partnerships and assisted sales.

---

## Business Context

The company aims to leverage customer data to better understand customer behavior, improve marketing effectiveness and increase customer value.

The analytics ecosystem supports different business questions, including:

- Who are our customers?
- Which products are most relevant for different customer segments?
- Which channels generate the highest-value customers?
- How can marketing campaigns be optimized?
- Which customers are more likely to purchase again?
- Which customers are at risk of churn?
- How can customer lifetime value be increased?

---

## Analytics Scope

The project explores different customer analytics areas across the customer lifecycle.

### Customer Overview & Business Performance

Understanding the customer base, product portfolio, sales channels and overall business performance.

### Customer Segmentation & Targeting

Identifying customer groups and supporting personalized marketing and commercial strategies.

### Campaign & Marketing Analytics

Analyzing campaign effectiveness, customer engagement, targeting and conversion performance.

### Customer Experience Analytics

Understanding customer interactions and, through future extensions of the data ecosystem, customer satisfaction and feedback.

### Customer Lifecycle Analytics

Analyzing purchase frequency, retention, churn patterns, cross-selling and customer value over time.

---

## Data Ecosystem

The project includes a synthetic data ecosystem designed to simulate a real-world insurance environment.

The initial data model includes information related to:

- Customers
- Products
- Sales channels
- Marketing campaigns
- Customer interactions
- Quotes
- Policies
- Renewals
- Campaign and customer operations

The different datasets are connected to reproduce the main stages of the customer lifecycle, from acquisition and marketing interaction to quote generation, policy purchase and renewal.

Additional data sources such as payments, claims, customer feedback, surveys and web interactions may be introduced in future extensions.

The dataset is designed as an evolving business asset, allowing new data sources, customers, campaigns and business scenarios to be progressively added over time.
