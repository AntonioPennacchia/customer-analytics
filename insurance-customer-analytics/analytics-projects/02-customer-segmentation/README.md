
# Wossist — Customer Segmentation & Profiling

## Project Overview

This project develops a multidimensional customer segmentation and profiling framework for **Wossist**, a fictional insurance company operating across Travel, Pet and Home insurance products.

Building on the Customer Base Overview project, the analysis focuses on understanding customer purchasing behaviour, product diversification, sales channel usage, lifecycle stages and marketing responsiveness.

The objective is to transform customer and transactional data into an explainable, customer-level analytical dataset that supports differentiated CRM and marketing analysis.

All datasets are synthetic.

## Business Question

**How can Wossist combine purchasing behaviour and customer characteristics to identify meaningful customer groups and support more relevant CRM actions?**

## Analytical Framework

The project is structured around two complementary analytical components.

### 01 — RFM Segmentation

RFM analysis evaluates customers with at least one recorded policy purchase using three transactional dimensions:

- **Recency:** days since the most recent policy purchase.
- **Frequency:** number of policies purchased.
- **Monetary Value:** total insurance premium generated by the customer.

The analysis includes exploratory data analysis, scoring methodology, customer segmentation and validation.

Scoring rules are adapted to the observed purchasing patterns of Wossist customers, producing seven interpretable RFM segments.

### 02 — Customer Business Profiling

RFM segmentation is complemented by four business-oriented profiling dimensions:

| Dimension | Analytical Focus |
|---|---|
| **Product Profiling** | Purchased business-line diversification and policy counts across six product variants |
| **Channel Profiling** | Purchasing-channel diversification and policy counts across four sales channels |
| **Lifecycle Profiling** | Registration, quotation and purchasing history |
| **Engagement Profiling** | Email responsiveness, telephone outcomes, Web advertising interactions and interaction recency |

Product, Channel and Lifecycle Profiling use mutually exclusive customer classifications.

Engagement Profiling instead retains independent numerical indicators, allowing responsiveness and interaction activity to be explored without introducing arbitrary engagement segments.

The framework covers both purchasing customers and registered users without policy purchases.

## Analytical Workflow

The project follows a modular workflow:

**Source Datasets → RFM Segmentation → Customer Business Profiling → Integrated Customer Dataset → Power BI**

The analysis is developed through two Python notebooks:

- `01-rfm-segmentation.ipynb` — RFM methodology, scoring, segmentation and validation.
- `02-customer-business-profiling.ipynb` — Product, Channel, Lifecycle and Engagement Profiling, followed by customer-level integration and validation.

Two analytical outputs are generated:

- `customer_rfm.csv.gz` — RFM metrics, scores and segments for purchasing customers.
- `customer_business_profiling.csv.gz` — integrated customer-level dataset containing business profiling attributes and RFM results.

The integrated dataset includes all registered customers, with RFM attributes populated only for customers with recorded purchases.

The original source datasets remain unchanged.

## Key Analytical Results

The final analytical dataset contains:

- **631,079 registered customers**, represented by unique customer identifiers.
- **198,073 purchasing customers (31.4%)**, with RFM segmentation.
- **433,006 non-purchasing customers (68.6%)**, retained for business profiling.
- **50 customer-level attributes**, combining customer characteristics, purchasing behaviour, profiling dimensions and marketing interactions.

The analysis highlights several characteristics of the Wossist customer base:

- Most purchasing customers have bought policies within a single insurance business line.
- Purchasing through multiple sales channels is relatively uncommon.
- Most purchasing customers have recorded only one policy purchase.
- Recorded marketing interactions are available for approximately one-quarter of the registered customer base.

These findings provide a foundation for exploring customer groups, identifying cross-selling opportunities and comparing purchasing behaviour with observed marketing responsiveness.

## Power BI Reporting & Case Studies

The integrated dataset is designed for interactive reporting in Power BI.

The report will bring together RFM segmentation, business profiling and dedicated CRM case studies, combining visual analysis with business interpretation directly within the reporting experience.

The customer-level dataset supports filtering and cross-analysis across product ownership, sales channels, lifecycle stages, customer characteristics and engagement indicators.
