# Wossist — Customer Segmentation & Profiling

## Project Overview

This project develops a multidimensional customer segmentation and profiling framework for **Wossist**, a fictional insurance company operating across Travel, Pet and Home insurance products.

Building on the Customer Base Overview project, the analysis focuses on understanding customer purchasing behaviour, product preferences, sales channels, lifecycle stages and marketing interactions.

The objective is to transform the available customer and transactional data into an explainable, customer-level analytical dataset that supports differentiated CRM and marketing decisions.

All datasets are synthetic.

## Business Question

**How can Wossist combine purchasing behaviour and customer characteristics to identify meaningful customer groups and support more relevant CRM actions?**

## Analytical Framework

The project is structured around two complementary analytical components.

### 01 — RFM Segmentation

RFM analysis evaluates Purchasing Customers using three transactional dimensions:

- **Recency:** days since the most recent policy purchase.
- **Frequency:** number of policies purchased.
- **Monetary Value:** total premium generated by the customer.

The analysis includes exploratory data analysis, scoring methodology, customer segmentation and validation.

Scoring rules are adapted to the observed purchasing patterns of Wossist customers.

### 02 — Customer Business Profiling

RFM segmentation is complemented by four business-oriented profiling dimensions:

| Dimension | Analytical Focus |
|---|---|
| **Product Profile** | Business line preferences, product concentration and multi-line purchasing |
| **Channel Profile** | Dominant sales channels and multi-channel purchasing behaviour |
| **Lifecycle Stage** | Registration, quotation and purchasing history |
| **Engagement Profile** | Interaction history, communication channels and observed responsiveness |

These dimensions enrich the transactional segmentation and allow customer groups to be analysed from multiple business perspectives.

The framework includes both Purchasing Customers and registered users without policy purchases.

## Analytical Workflow

The project follows a modular workflow:

**Source Datasets → RFM Segmentation → Customer Business Profiling → Integrated Customer Dataset → Power BI**

The analysis is developed through two Python notebooks:

- `01-rfm-segmentation.ipynb` — RFM methodology, scoring, segmentation and validation.
- `02-customer-business-profiling.ipynb` — complementary business profiling and integration of customer-level features.

Two analytical outputs are generated:

- `customer_rfm.csv.gz` — customer-level RFM metrics, scores and segments.
- `customer_segmentation.csv.gz` — integrated customer-level dataset, including RFM results and business profiling dimensions.

The original source datasets remain unchanged.

## Power BI Reporting & Case Studies

The integrated dataset is designed for interactive reporting in Power BI.

The report brings together customer segmentation, business profiling and dedicated CRM case studies, combining visual analysis with business interpretation directly within the reporting experience.
