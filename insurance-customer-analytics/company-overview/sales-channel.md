# Sales & Customer Engagement Channels

## Overview

Wossist interacts with customers through different sales and engagement channels.

Sales channels describe how insurance products are purchased, while customer engagement channels describe how Wossist communicates with customers and prospects through marketing and customer relationship activities.

The distinction between these two concepts allows Wossist to analyze both commercial performance and customer engagement effectiveness.

---

# Sales Channels

## Website

The Wossist website represents the main digital direct sales channel.

Customers can independently explore insurance products, request a quote and complete the purchase process online.

The website channel allows Wossist to analyze:

- Digital acquisition performance
- Online conversion rates
- Customer behavior during the purchase journey

---

## Agency

Agencies represent a traditional assisted sales channel where customers interact directly with insurance consultants.

Agents support customers in understanding products, selecting coverage options and completing the purchase process.

The agency channel allows Wossist to analyze:

- Agency performance
- Geographic distribution of sales
- Customer value by agency channel

---

## Partner

Partners are external organizations that collaborate with Wossist to distribute insurance products.

Examples include travel-related companies, commercial partners or other businesses that introduce customers to Wossist solutions.

The partner channel allows Wossist to analyze:

- Partner contribution to customer acquisition
- Product performance by partner
- Customer quality generated by partnerships

---

## Assisted Sales

Assisted Sales includes sales processes where Wossist employees directly support customers during the purchase journey.

Examples include:

- Phone-based sales activities
- Customer support teams supporting conversion
- Outbound sales campaigns

This channel allows Wossist to analyze:

- Effectiveness of sales interactions
- Conversion after customer contact
- Value generated by assisted sales activities

---

# Customer Engagement Channels

## Email

Email campaigns are used to communicate with customers and prospects.

Examples include:

- Promotional campaigns
- Product recommendations
- Renewal reminders
- Customer communications

Email interactions can include events such as:

- Email sent
- Email opened
- Link clicked

---

## Phone

Phone interactions represent direct conversations between Wossist and customers.

Phone activities can be used for:

- Targeted sales campaigns
- Customer retention activities
- Customer support interactions

Phone campaigns are particularly relevant when customer selection is based on analytical models, such as customer segmentation or RFM analysis.

---

## SMS

SMS communications are used for short and timely customer messages.

Examples include:

- Reminders
- Notifications
- Promotional messages

---

## Digital Marketing

Digital marketing includes online activities aimed at acquiring new customers or increasing product awareness.

Examples include:

- Search advertising
- Social media campaigns
- Display advertising

Digital marketing activities help Wossist analyze customer acquisition sources and marketing effectiveness.
