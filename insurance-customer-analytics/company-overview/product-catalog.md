# Product Catalog

## Overview

Wossist offers insurance products designed to protect customers across different areas of their personal life.

The product portfolio is structured around three main business lines:

- Travel Insurance
- Pet Insurance
- Home Insurance

Each product line includes different coverage options and customer segments.

---

# Travel Insurance

Travel Insurance is one of Wossist's core business areas, providing protection for customers during domestic and international trips.

The product line includes both occasional and recurring travel solutions.

## Products

### Single Trip Travel Insurance

A short-term insurance product designed for customers travelling for a specific journey.

Typical use cases:

- Holidays
- Business trips
- International travel

Key characteristics:

- One-time purchase
- Trip-specific coverage
- Flexible duration

---

### Annual Travel Insurance

A recurring travel solution designed for customers who travel multiple times during the year.

Typical customers:

- Frequent travellers
- Business travellers
- Customers with international mobility needs

Key characteristics:

- Annual subscription
- Multiple trips coverage
- Higher customer value compared to single-trip products

---

# Pet Insurance

Pet Insurance provides protection solutions for customers who own dogs and cats.

The product line is designed to support customers with veterinary expenses and pet-related risks.

## Products

### Pet Standard

An entry-level product providing essential coverage.

Typical characteristics:

- Basic veterinary protection
- Affordable premium
- High accessibility

---

### Pet Premium

An enhanced product designed for customers looking for broader protection.

Typical characteristics:

- Higher coverage limits
- Additional services
- Higher premium value

---

# Home Insurance

Home Insurance provides protection for residential properties and household risks.

The product line targets customers looking for protection against unexpected events affecting their home.

## Products

### Home Standard

A basic protection solution covering essential household risks.

Typical characteristics:

- Core coverage
- Competitive pricing
- Suitable for a broad customer base

---

### Home Premium

A more comprehensive protection solution designed for customers requiring extended coverage.

Typical characteristics:

- Additional protections
- Higher coverage limits
- Higher customer value
