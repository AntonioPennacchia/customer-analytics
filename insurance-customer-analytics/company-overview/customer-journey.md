# Customer Journey

## Overview

The customer journey describes the main stages through which a customer interacts with Wossist, from the first marketing interaction to the long-term relationship with the company.

Understanding the customer lifecycle allows Wossist to analyze customer behavior, optimize marketing activities and improve customer retention.

---

# Customer Lifecycle

The Wossist customer journey is structured into the following stages:

1. Customer Acquisition
2. Customer Engagement
3. Quote Generation
4. Policy Purchase
5. Customer Relationship
6. Renewal & Retention

---

# 1. Customer Acquisition

The customer enters the Wossist ecosystem through different acquisition channels.

Customers may discover Wossist through:

- Digital marketing activities
- Partner channels
- Website visits
- Agency interactions

At this stage, the company focuses on understanding where new customers come from and which channels generate valuable customers.

Potential analytics:

- New customer acquisition
- Channel performance
- Customer profile analysis

---

# 2. Customer Engagement

Wossist interacts with customers and prospects through different communication channels.

Main engagement channels include:

- Email campaigns
- Phone interactions
- SMS communications
- Digital marketing activities

Customer interactions are tracked to understand customer interest and campaign effectiveness.

Examples of recorded interactions:

- Email sent
- Email opened
- Link clicked
- Phone call completed
- Customer response

---

# 3. Quote Generation

Interested customers can request a quote for an insurance product.

A quote represents an explicit customer interest before purchasing a policy.

The quote process allows Wossist to analyze:

- Which products generate more interest
- Quote-to-purchase conversion
- Customer drop-off points
- Time between quote and purchase

---

# 4. Policy Purchase

When a customer completes the purchase process, a new insurance policy is created.

Each policy records information such as:

- Customer
- Product
- Purchase date
- Sales channel
- Premium value
- Campaign attribution (when available)

Sales channel and marketing attribution are considered separate concepts:

- Sales channel describes how the policy was purchased.
- Campaign attribution identifies the marketing activity that influenced the purchase when this information is available.

---

# 5. Customer Relationship

After purchase, customers enter an ongoing relationship with Wossist.

During this stage, the company analyzes:

- Customer portfolio
- Product ownership
- Customer value
- Cross-selling opportunities
- Customer engagement

Examples:

- Travel customer interested in Pet Insurance
- Standard customer eligible for Premium upgrade

---

# 6. Renewal & Retention

For recurring insurance products, renewal represents a key moment in the customer lifecycle.

Wossist monitors customer behavior to identify:

- Renewal rate
- Customers at risk of leaving
- Retention opportunities

Retention activities may include:

- Renewal campaigns
- Personalized offers
- Customer engagement activities

---

Each stage of the customer journey generates analytical data:

Customer
↓
Customer Interaction
↓
Quote
↓
Policy
↓
Renewal

These events represent the foundation of the Wossist customer analytics ecosystem.
