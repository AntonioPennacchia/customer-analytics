# Business Context

## Company Overview

**Wossist** is a fictional insurance and assistance company created to simulate a real-world customer analytics environment.

The company operates in the retail insurance market, providing digital protection solutions designed to support customers across different moments of their lives.

Wossist focuses on combining insurance products, customer assistance services and data-driven decision making to improve customer experience and business performance.

---

## Business Model

Wossist generates revenue by offering insurance products to retail customers through different acquisition and distribution channels.

The company operates across three main business areas:

- Travel Insurance
- Pet Insurance
- Home Insurance

Each business line includes different products and coverage options designed to meet specific customer needs.

---

## Target Customers

Wossist serves individual customers looking for flexible and accessible protection solutions.

The customer base includes different segments based on:

- Demographic characteristics
- Lifestyle and personal needs
- Product preferences
- Purchase behavior
- Customer engagement level

---
## Sales & Distribution Channels

Customers can purchase Wossist products through multiple sales channels, including:

- Website
- Agency
- Partner
- Assisted Sales

Each channel represents where the customer completes the quote and purchase process.

Sales channels are distinct from marketing and customer engagement channels, which describe how customers are acquired, contacted or influenced before purchase.

Understanding the contribution and performance of each channel is a key component of the customer analytics ecosystem.
---

## Business Objectives

The company aims to leverage customer analytics to:

- Improve customer acquisition strategies
- Increase customer engagement
- Optimize marketing campaigns
- Improve retention and renewal rates
- Identify cross-selling opportunities
- Increase customer lifetime value

---

## Analytics Perspective

From a customer analytics perspective, Wossist data can be used to understand the complete customer lifecycle:

Customer Acquisition  
→ Customer Activation  
→ Product Usage  
→ Engagement  
→ Renewal  
→ Long-term Customer Value
