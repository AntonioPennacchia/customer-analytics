# Customer Analytics Portfolio

This repository contains customer analytics projects focused on understanding customer behavior and transforming data into actionable business insights.

The goal is to demonstrate how customer data can support data-driven decisions across the customer lifecycle, from acquisition and engagement to retention and customer value.

The projects combine:

- Business understanding
- Data modeling
- Customer analytics
- Segmentation analysis
- Marketing analytics
- Reporting and dashboarding

## Current Case Study

The first case study focuses on **Insurance Customer Analytics**, simulating a real-world insurance business environment.

The project explores how customer data can be structured, analyzed and transformed into insights supporting business decisions across products, channels and customer journeys.

## Analytics Areas

The repository explores different areas of customer analytics, including:

- Customer overview and business performance
- Customer segmentation
- Campaign targeting and marketing performance
- Customer satisfaction analysis
- Customer lifecycle analysis
- Retention and churn analysis
- Customer value analysis

## Approach

Each case study follows a business-oriented analytics approach:

1. Understanding the business context
2. Designing the data ecosystem
3. Preparing and structuring data
4. Analyzing customer behavior
5. Creating insights and dashboards to support decision making
